## Individual Product Identity System

**Status:** Product identity architecture  
**Scope:** Approved 50-product draft catalog  
**Source of truth:** WP-CAT-01, WP-CAT-02, WP-CAT-03, WP-CAT-04, WP-DES-CAT-02, and WP-DES-CAT-03A  
**Implementation status:** Documentation only  
**Images, storefront, Shopware entities, database, templates, styles, scripts, routes, search, checkout, and governance:** Unchanged

---

## Purpose

This document defines how each approved product should be perceived as an
individual member of the Veylune catalog. It does not define product design,
storefront design, image production, merchandising implementation, or public
exposure.

The identity system uses:

- **Product character** to define the product's dominant formal impression.
- **Emotional tone** to define the feeling it should carry.
- **Material priority** to identify the material that leads perception.
- **Spatial role** to define the product's presence relative to a room.
- **Collection role** to explain its approved collection relationship.
- **Room role** to explain its approved room relationship.
- **Luxury positioning** to express its catalog-relative role: Entry, Core,
  Premium, or Statement.

Luxury positioning is an identity classification based on the existing catalog
position, target price context, hierarchy, material authority, scale, and
Founder Selection potential. It is not a pricing approval.

# 2. Collection Alignment Review

## Quiet Living

**Aligned products:** F01, F02, F03, F04, F05, F07, F08, F09, F11, F14,
F15, F16, F17, D05, T01, T02, T03, T04, T05.

The collection is coherent around calm habitation, warm materials, tactile
comfort, low visual noise, and domestic utility. Its strongest identity anchors
are F01, F15, D05, and T01. F05, F08, T03, T04, and T05 operate correctly as
supporting domestic layers rather than signature objects.

**Observation:** The collection contains many wood-and-soft-material products.
Individuality therefore depends on preserving clear differences between
architectural foundations, crafted frames, and textile accents.

## Material Forms

**Aligned products:** F06, F10, F12, F13, F18, F19, L06, D01, D02, D03, D04,
D06, D07, D08, D09, K01.

The collection is coherent around material-led identity: stone mass, marble
veining, travertine pores, leather drape, glass transparency, metal precision,
ceramic glaze, and deliberate junctions. F10, F13, D04, D08, and K01 provide
the strongest range of scale and form.

**Observation:** D01, D02, D03, and D08 share a concentrated stone-object
territory. Their identities remain distinct only when artisan irregularity,
geological layering, architectural precision, and monolithic mass are treated
as separate perceptions.

## Architectural Light

**Aligned products:** L01, L02, L03, L04, L05, L07, L08, L09, L10.

The collection is coherent around fixture silhouette, controlled glow, spatial
definition, and restrained technical resolution. L02 and L04 are the principal
brand representatives; L01, L05, and L08 provide secondary architectural
authority; L03, L07, L09, and L10 add domestic scale.

**Observation:** L03 and L07 are the closest pair. Lumen must remain rounded
and object-like, while Vela remains tapered, softer, and more intimate.

## Table Rituals

**Aligned products:** K02, K03, K04.

The collection is coherent around composed dining behavior rather than
decoration. Ceramic tactility, wood service, and metal order provide adequate
material contrast despite the collection's small size.

**Observation:** The collection is intentionally supporting rather than
hero-led. Its identity depends on ritual, handling, and function.

## Open Air

**Aligned products:** O01, O02, O03.

The collection is coherent as a complete exterior micro-system: seating,
surface, and planting structure. Wood warmth, stone mass, and planted vertical
rhythm provide clear internal differentiation.

**Observation:** O01 and O02 carry most of the collection's authority. O03 is
important as a supporting environmental object, not as an equal visual hero.

## Founder Selection

**Assigned products:** F01, F03, F10, F13, F14, F15, L02, L04, D03, D04, D05,
T01, K01, O01, O02.

The assigned set is coherent with Veylune's strongest signals: architectural
form, material authority, quiet comfort, and collectible presence. The set
contains sufficient departmental range and does not depend on one room or one
material.

# 3. Room Alignment Review

## Living Room

The room has the catalog's strongest identity coverage. Foundation products
F01, F02, F10, F11, F16, and T01 are balanced by individual seating, lighting,
side tables, and objects. The main overlap risk is not room relevance but the
density of stone objects and occasional tables.

## Dining Room

K01 provides a strong center, supported by F05, F06, F08, K02, K03, and K04.
F09, F16, vessels, and lighting extend the room without confusing the primary
dining hierarchy. The room reads as composed and material-led rather than
tableware-led.

## Bedroom

F15 and D05 provide clear architectural anchors. Lounge seating, bedside
tables, portable and wall lighting, rugs, throws, and cushions build a complete
quiet-luxury environment. F03, F04, and F18 must retain different postures so
the room does not present three interchangeable occasional chairs.

## Home Office

F14 and F07 establish the work function. F17 adds storage, while L01, L02, L07,
D04, and D09 create vertical, luminous, and collectible layers. The room is
coherent, though its strongest products also belong elsewhere; the desk and
desk chair must remain its identity anchors.

## Hallway

F09, F13, D05, and T02 provide strong linear and vertical architecture.
Lighting, vessels, D06, D08, and O03 add support at different scales. The room
has a clear threshold identity, with F13 and D05 as its strongest
representatives.

## Outdoor

O01, O02, and O03 form a concise but complete room proposition. Their seating,
surface, and planting roles do not overlap. The room's identity is coherent and
materially balanced.

All 50 products can belong credibly to Veylune. No product is fundamentally
inconsistent with the brand's quiet architectural luxury, material-first
presentation, warm restraint, and gallery-like object treatment.
