## Current Bottleneck

Milestone 8 is the critical-path blocker. It cannot be completed from repository
planning facts, product names, legacy prices, existing images, or Phase 9 mock
suppliers. Required external inputs begin with Aurelia and Calma and include
legal supplier identity, supplier SKU, batch, price and availability authority,
specification pack, media-rights schedule, material evidence, source owner, and
reviewer.
