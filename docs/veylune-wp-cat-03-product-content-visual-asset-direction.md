# VEYLUNE STUDIO

## WP-CAT-03 — Product Content & Visual Asset Direction

**Status:** Governed content and visual production blueprint  
**Scope:** All 50 WP-CAT-01 and WP-CAT-02 product records  
**Implementation authority:** None  
**Catalog authority:** WP-CAT-01 and WP-CAT-02 remain the source of truth

---

## 1. Purpose

This document converts the 50 governed product records into content and visual
production briefs. It does not create products, approve supplier claims, grant
collection membership, or authorize storefront exposure.

All names, descriptions, material stories, features, images, rail placements,
and Founder Selection assessments remain drafts until the relevant product,
supplier, material, media, collection, exposure, and founder authorities
approve them.

## 2. Content Governance

- Commerce descriptions must remain factual, concise, and product-first.
- Material stories describe the intended design proposition, not verified
  composition. Supplier specifications or physical inspection must confirm
  every material claim before publication.
- German names are localization drafts. Bilingual content review is required.
- Features are acquisition requirements. Unsupported features must be removed,
  not converted into softer marketing language.
- `Coming Soon` is reserved for the four known product pipeline records.
- `Supplier Selection` identifies planned archetypes without an approved source.
- Rail placement is a merchandising candidate, never exposure approval.
- `Available Now` is excluded until sellability and availability are verified.
- Founder Selection potential means `High`, `Medium`, or `Low`; it does not
  confer Level 4 eligibility or founder selection.

## 3. Visual System

Every future media set still requires at least five rights-cleared images. This
document directs the three images that establish the product's commercial
identity:

1. **Primary:** Product-dominant, complete silhouette, calm warm-neutral
   background, consistent three-quarter or front elevation, no decorative
   props competing with the object.
2. **Detail:** The material, construction, finish, junction, edge, weave, or
   functional mechanism that substantiates the product proposition.
3. **Scale/context:** Credible room placement with circulation and human or
   familiar-object scale where useful; context must not imply an unsupported
   room, installation, durability, or use claim.

Lighting requires illuminated and off-state coverage. Natural stone, wood,
leather, ceramic, and textile products must show honest variation. Outdoor
imagery requires documented outdoor suitability before public use.
