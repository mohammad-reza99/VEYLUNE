## 14. Evidence Acceptance Rules

- A current supplier specification, certificate, BOM, commercial confirmation,
  or approved physical verification is acceptable according to the existing
  confidence rules.
- Images, names, price, category, and prose are not material evidence.
- Availability must identify source, timestamp, policy and applicable variant.
- Pricing must identify gross, net, tax, currency, validity and approver.
- Media rights must identify owner, scope, territory/channel, modification/crop
  permission, expiry and each covered asset.
- Contradictory evidence blocks the product; reviewers do not choose the most
  convenient version.
