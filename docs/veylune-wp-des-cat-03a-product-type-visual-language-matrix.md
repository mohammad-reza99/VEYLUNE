# VEYLUNE STUDIO - WP-DES-CAT-03A

## Product Type Visual Language Matrix

**Status:** Visual architecture  
**Scope:** Existing 50-product draft catalog inventory  
**Source of truth:** WP-OP-01B product-type registry, WP-CAT-03 product inventory, and WP-DES-CAT-02 visual identity system  
**Implementation status:** Documentation only  
**Images, storefront, Shopware entities, database, and code:** Unchanged

---

## 1. Purpose

This document assigns one visual language to each approved product type represented
in the existing catalog inventory. It defines the communication intent of four
image roles without commissioning, generating, selecting, or publishing images.

The matrix does not change product taxonomy, hierarchy, merchandising, storefront
presentation, supplier status, product readiness, or exposure eligibility.

## 2. Image Role Contract

| Image role | Governing intent | Must answer | Must not substitute for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero | Establish immediate product-type recognition and the product's defining silhouette | What is the product, and what makes its form distinct? | Lifestyle atmosphere, hidden construction evidence, or surface-only study |
| Lifestyle | Establish credible use, scale, placement, and spatial relationship | Where and how does the product belong in use? | Editorial room styling or unsupported performance claims |
| Detail | Explain construction, function, junctions, edges, mechanisms, or product-defining features | How is the product resolved or used? | A generic close crop with no product-specific information |
| Material | Make the primary material and important secondary material behavior legible | What does the surface, finish, weave, grain, patina, translucency, or variation communicate? | Supplier verification or proof of an unapproved material claim |

### Separation Rule

Detail and material images may share a crop only when the same view clearly
communicates both construction and material behavior. The two intents remain
separate acceptance criteria.

## 5. Architecture Acceptance Criteria

The matrix is complete when:

- each of the 50 existing products resolves to one approved product type;
- each product type has distinct hero, lifestyle, detail, and material intents;
- hero intent prioritizes recognition rather than atmosphere;
- lifestyle intent explains use and scale rather than decoration;
- detail intent identifies construction, function, or product-specific resolution;
- material intent communicates surface behavior without asserting unverified facts;
- related products can share a type language without becoming visually interchangeable;
- the matrix remains independent of image production and storefront implementation.

## Governance Boundary

This document does not:

- generate, commission, select, edit, or approve images;
- prescribe storefront layout, components, routes, merchandising, or interaction;
- modify code, Shopware configuration, product records, taxonomy, or exposure;
- validate supplier materials, construction, compliance, durability, or availability;
- authorize public use of any future asset.
